How to spot the Sombrero Galaxy from a dark-sky site

A bright galaxy with a dark lane across its middle is within reach of a modest backyard telescope.

By Elin Marchetti · November 3, 2026

The Sombrero Galaxy gets its name from its shape: a bright, round centre with a thin brim of dark dust around it. Seen from the right angle, it really does look like a hat.

To find it, travel to a dark site away from town lights and let your eyes adapt. A telescope with an eight-inch mirror shows a pale oval glow with a hint of the dark lane.

Tips for a first sighting

Patience matters more than power. Use a low-power eyepiece to find the galaxy, then switch to a medium one and look slightly to the side of the glow, which uses the more sensitive part of your eye.

Bring a red torch, warm layers and a notebook. Sketching what you see, even badly, trains your eye and makes the next observation easier.
