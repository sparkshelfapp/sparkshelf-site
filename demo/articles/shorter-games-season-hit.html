Players say shorter games are the real hit of the season

Five-hour adventures are finding devoted audiences among people with busy weeks.

By Rosa Delacroix · November 5, 2026

A poll of 2,000 invented players by the Pixel Post readers' panel found that more than half had finished more games this year than last. The reason was simple: the games were shorter.

Studios have noticed. Adventures that wrap up in a weekend, puzzle games built around a single clever idea, and story games with one strong ending are all enjoying loyal followings.

Players describe the relief of finishing something. "I used to buy huge games and never get past the first town," said reader Maya Okafor. "Now I actually see the credits."

Not everyone is sold. Fans of long role-playing adventures say they still want hundreds of hours, and publishers insist there will be room for both.
