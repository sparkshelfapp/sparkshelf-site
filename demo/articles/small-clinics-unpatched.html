Small clinics are hit hardest by unpatched servers, survey finds

A new report says the biggest risk is not clever hackers but updates that nobody has time to install.

By Samuel Okoye · November 6, 2026

A survey of 300 small clinics in the invented Aldermoor region found that more than half run at least one server with known, fixable flaws. The cause was rarely carelessness. It was time.

Most clinics have no dedicated IT staff. Updates wait for a quiet afternoon that never comes, and nobody wants to restart the booking system on a busy morning.

The report's authors suggest a small, repeatable routine: one fixed hour each month, a short checklist, and a shared calendar so that the whole team knows when the system will restart.

Some regional associations are going further and offering shared maintenance staff. "A clinic should not need to hire an engineer to keep its front door locked," said survey lead Ingrid Falk.
