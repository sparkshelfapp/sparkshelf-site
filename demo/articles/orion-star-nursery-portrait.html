Nursery of new stars gets its sharpest portrait yet

A combined image reveals the glowing gas and dust where hundreds of young stars are being born.

By Dara Whitcombe · November 5, 2026

A new composite picture of the famous stellar nursery in the constellation Orion shows swirls of gas in shades of rust, teal and violet, with young stars shining through the dust.

The image combines observations from several telescopes, each tuned to a different kind of light. Visible light shows the glowing gas, while infrared light lets us see through the dust to stars that are still forming.

Teachers have already asked for the picture. The invented Orbit Education Office says it will release a free poster, a worksheet and a short guide for classes of all ages.

For observers, the nebula is also a friendly target. On a dark night it is visible to the naked eye as a faint fuzzy patch, and binoculars show its glowing heart.
