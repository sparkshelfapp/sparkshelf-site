Delete your Sparkshelf account

Sparkshelf (com.sparkshelf.app)

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Sparkshelf → Settings → Account.
  2. Tap Delete my account and cloud data and confirm. Google may ask you to sign in again first.

This deletes your cloud copy (saved links, tags, flags, reading progress, highlights and notes, AI summaries, news topics and feeds, app settings and encrypted API keys if you had switched those on, the daily counter of free built-in summaries, the monthly counter of built-in listening and your Premium status record) and your sign-in account immediately and permanently. There is no waiting period and it cannot be undone or restored. The library of that account on your phone is removed as well; libraries of other accounts on the same phone are not touched.

Delete only some of your data (keep your account)

  1. Open Sparkshelf → Settings → Account → What to sync.
  2. Tap Remove from the cloud…, tick what you want removed (saved articles, highlights and notes, AI summaries, news topics and feeds, app settings, API keys) and confirm.

The cloud copy of each ticked kind is deleted immediately and permanently, and that kind stops syncing. Your account stays, and the data on your phone is not touched. You can also simply switch a kind off to stop syncing it without deleting its cloud copy.

By email

If you can no longer open the app, email sparkshelfapp@gmail.com from the address of the Google account you used, with the subject "Delete my Sparkshelf account". We will delete your data and confirm by reply, normally within a few days and in any case within 30 days. The 30 days is only the time we allow ourselves to process your request; it is not a period in which your data can be restored. Once deleted, it is gone for good.

What is stored and why is explained in the Privacy Policy.
