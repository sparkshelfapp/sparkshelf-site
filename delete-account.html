Delete your Sparkshelf account

Sparkshelf (com.sparkshelf.app)

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Sparkshelf → Settings → Account.
  2. Tap Delete my account and cloud data and confirm. Google may ask you to sign in again first.

This permanently deletes your cloud copy (saved links, tags, flags, reading progress, AI summaries, news topics and feeds) and your sign-in account. Articles already on your phone stay there until you delete them or uninstall the app.

By email

If you can no longer open the app, email sparkshelfapp@gmail.com from the address of the Google account you used, with the subject "Delete my Sparkshelf account". We will delete your data and confirm by reply, normally within 30 days.

What is stored and why is explained in the Privacy Policy.
