Welcome to Sparkshelf

Sparkshelf keeps what you want to read in one calm place. This guide is a normal article, so try everything on it, then delete it when you’re done.

Save anything

Share a link to Sparkshelf from any app, or tap + to paste one. PDF and EPUB files work too.

Read your way

Articles open as clean pages, even offline. Tap Tt to change the size, font and theme.

Highlight and add notes

Press and hold a sentence and choose Highlight. Tap a highlight to write a note.

Summarize with AI

Tap Summarize for a short summary, or the spark for Wisdom, Fact check and Chat.

Listen

Tap the headphones to hear any article. Choose a voice in Settings → AI providers.

Build your News

In News, add the topics you follow and your own RSS feeds, or import a friend’s list from Settings → News sources.

Yours to keep

Everything stays on your phone. Sign in only if you want your library on other devices.

That’s it. You can replay the app tour any time from Settings → App guide.
