Sparkshelf Privacy Policy

Effective 10 October 2026 · App: Sparkshelf (com.sparkshelf.app) · Published by BingoWorks · Contact: sparkshelfapp@gmail.com

In short. Sparkshelf keeps your reading library on your phone. If you choose to sign in, a copy of your library details is stored in your private cloud account so it can sync between your devices. Article text and your API keys never leave your phone for our servers. Sparkshelf has no analytics, no crash-reporting service and no advertising, and we do not sell your data.

1. What stays on your phone

  • The articles you save (link, title, the downloaded article text and pictures for offline reading), your tags, favourites, archive and read marks, reading progress, and the AI summaries you generate.
  • Your settings (appearance, reminders, reading and voice options, app lock choice).
  • Your API keys for AI services, if you add any. They are stored encrypted with the Android Keystore, only on this phone.

This data is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data. Android's automatic backup is configured to exclude your API keys and your sign-in/sync state.

2. If you sign in (optional)

Signing in is optional and only needed for syncing. We use Google Sign-In through Firebase Authentication (Google LLC). We receive your Google account's name, email address, a unique account ID and your profile-picture address. The picture is loaded from Google each time it is shown and is not stored by us.

When you are signed in, the following is stored in Cloud Firestore (Google Cloud), in a space only your account can read and write:

  • the links and titles of your saved articles, their tags, favourite/archive/read marks and reading progress;
  • your AI summaries;
  • your news topics and the RSS feed addresses you added.

Never uploaded: article text, downloaded pictures, your API keys, and your settings. Google processes this data for us as a service provider under its own Firebase privacy terms and Privacy Policy. We use it only to provide sync.

3. Services you connect yourself

Some features send content to a third-party service that you choose, only when you use them and only with an API key you enter yourself. This content goes directly from your phone to that provider. It is not sent to us, and we never receive, see or store it.

FeatureWhat is sentSent to (the provider you choose, not to us)
SummarizeThe article's title and textThe provider you pick: Anthropic, OpenAI, Google (Gemini), or a compatible service whose address you enter
Read aloud with a cloud voiceThe text being readGoogle (Gemini), OpenAI or ElevenLabs, whichever voice you choose

Your key and the content go only to that provider. The provider's own terms and privacy policy apply, and you may be billed by them for your usage. The phone's built-in voice works entirely on the phone. The "Ask Gemini" shortcut hands the article text to the Gemini app installed on your phone, which is then governed by Google's terms. You can remove a key at any time in Settings.

4. Websites and feeds the app contacts

  • When you save a link, the app downloads that page from its website to extract the article. That website can see your phone's IP address and standard request details, like any browser visit.
  • The News tab downloads RSS/Atom feeds from the news sources listed in Settings and from feed addresses you add, and loads article thumbnails from those sites. Only each feed's own title, short snippet, tags and link are shown.
  • Opening an article from News fetches that page in the same way, and it is kept on your phone for up to 30 days so it opens faster offline.

5. Permissions

  • Internet: to download articles and feeds, sync, and use the services you connect.
  • Notifications (Android 13+): reading reminders and the read-aloud player controls. Optional.
  • Foreground service (media playback): keeps read-aloud playing when the screen is off.
  • The optional app lock uses your phone's own screen lock through Android's prompt; Sparkshelf never sees or stores your PIN, pattern or biometrics.

6. What we don't do

  • No analytics and no crash-reporting service are built into the app.
  • No advertising is shown in the app today. If that ever changes, we will update this policy first and ask for consent where the law requires it.
  • We do not sell your personal data or share it for advertising.

7. Your choices, access and deletion

  • Delete your account and cloud data: Settings → Account → "Delete my account and cloud data". This removes everything stored in the cloud for you and deletes your sign-in account. Your articles remain on the phone. You can also request deletion by email (see Delete your account).
  • Export: Settings → Import & backup → "Export a backup" saves your library details, tags and summaries to a file you control.
  • Sign out at any time; your phone keeps its local copy.
  • Depending on where you live (for example under the EU/UK GDPR or California law) you may have rights to access, correct, delete or port your data, and to object or complain to a regulator. Use the in-app tools above or contact us.

We keep cloud data only while your account exists. Deleting your account is immediate and permanent: we cannot restore it afterwards. Google may take some further time to purge residual copies from its own backup systems under its standard deletion schedule; they are not accessible to us or to the app.

8. Children

Sparkshelf is not directed at children under 13 (or the higher age required in your country), and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

9. Security

Connections use HTTPS wherever the site supports it. Cloud data is protected by account-based access rules so each user can reach only their own space, and API keys are encrypted on the device. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your phone and Google account protected.

10. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
